Last time I was in Tokyo, I decided on a whim to visit some locations from Kara no Kyōkai, one of my favorite anime movies. Also it is made by Kinoko Nasu, the author of Tsukihime, which I also like a lot (and Fate, of course).

Since there are tons of places spread throughout Tokyo (and one in Osaka) and I only had roughly one hour I decided to visit the bridge from the first movie, which I found quite memorable. Also I had wanted to visit it because of the Kirin statues there anyway.

Another cool place closeby, is the clock outside of the Yurakucho Mullion. It was the first large marionette clock in Japan and was installed in 1984. Every full hour between 10:00 to 22:00 it moves up to reveal four golden figures giving a small musical performance. It was featured in the fifth Kara no Kyōkai movie.

Due to my time constraints - it was almost midnight and my last day in Japan - I sadly wasn't able to watch the clock's performance, but I will come back to watch it one day.
